Each page names the session, the year and the city, links straight to the slides
where they are public, and carries the services behind the build, the longer
write-up and the sources.

Slides are free to read, use and share, with no email needed. If you reuse
something, please credit the source. Where a deck is not public yet, the page
says so rather than hiding the talk.

23 events from 2022 to 2026, newest first. The same pages live on
[lindamohamed.com/talks](https://www.lindamohamed.com/talks), where they were
first published.
