## On the programme

### Generiert, verzerrt, unterrichtet

*2026 · Vienna*

The opening keynote at #11. Five minutes on deepfakes using clips I generated of myself, then bias in AI models, then the main block on how teachers and students actually use AI. Forty-three sources, each linked on its slide.
