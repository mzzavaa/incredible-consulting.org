**Purpose.** Session three is the one most engagements skip, which is why so many end in a shelved report. It exists to force a recorded decision with a name attached.

**Contains.**
- Non-negotiable entry condition: the person who can say yes is in the room
- Decision memo template with recommendation, cost, risk, and reversal cost
- Presentation order that puts the recommendation first and the evidence second
- Handling the "let us think about it" outcome as a scheduled decision, not a delay
- Costed plan format for the first ninety days
- Post-decision handover, including what moves to the commercial arm if paid delivery is chosen

**Primary CTA.** Get a signature or a scheduled date, never an open ending.
