**Purpose.** The practical download bundle for facilitators. For internal architects, other consultancies, and lecturers who want to run the method themselves.

**Contains.**
- Download table: agenda packs, worksheets, and board templates per workshop
- Slide skeletons with no branding, intended to be re-skinned
- Prompt library for the model-assisted parts of discovery and trade-off analysis
- Timing card, a single printable page for the facilitator
- Common facilitation failures and the recovery move for each
- Attribution note: what to keep in the footer when reusing the kit

**Primary CTA.** Download the kit and run it, attribution is the only requirement.
