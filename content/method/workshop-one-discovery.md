**Purpose.** Session one exists to stop the project solving the wrong problem. It is the cheapest place to kill a bad idea.

**Contains.**
- Agenda with timeboxes for each of the five blocks
- Opening exercise: current state described without any solution language
- Constraint harvesting: regulatory, data, budget, timeline, and political constraints
- Problem statement template and the criteria for a good one
- Success measure definition, including the baseline that must be captured now
- Facilitator notes on handling the dominant voice and the absent decision maker
- Output pack that goes to participants within twenty-four hours

**Primary CTA.** Run the constraint harvest, it is the highest-value forty minutes of the day.
