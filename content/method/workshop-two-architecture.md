**Purpose.** Session two turns the problem statement into options. Producing more than one option is the point, because a single option is a proposal, not a decision.

**Contains.**
- Preparation required from the facilitator between session one and two
- Candidate generation using the reference architecture library as a starting deck
- Trade-off matrix dimensions: cost, time to value, operational load, reversibility, risk
- Explicit "cheapest viable" and "most reversible" candidates as mandatory entries
- Open questions log with named owners and answer-by dates
- Facilitator notes on preventing premature vendor commitment

**Primary CTA.** Force a second candidate into the room before scoring anything.
