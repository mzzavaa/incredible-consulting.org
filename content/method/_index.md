**Purpose.** The methodology section explains the operating system behind the playbooks. It is written so another consultancy or an internal team can run it without us.

**Contains.**
- One-paragraph statement of the method and the outcome it commits to
- The three workshops as a sequence with inputs, outputs, and elapsed time
- Roles: who must be in the room and who must not
- What is deliberately excluded from the method and why
- Facilitation kit index: agendas, timeboxes, templates, and prompts
- Adaptation notes for remote, hybrid, and public sector settings

**Primary CTA.** Read the three-workshop method and schedule workshop one.
