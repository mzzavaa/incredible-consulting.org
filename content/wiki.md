[ai-solutions.wiki](https://ai-solutions.wiki/) is a free, open knowledge base
for building AI systems that survive production. Most of it is reference
material: tools, comparisons, a glossary. The pieces below are different. They
started as my own talks, workshops, videos and projects, and were written down
so that nobody needs to have been in the room.

The wiki content is licensed
[CC BY-NC-SA 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-nc-sa/4.0/) and the
source is on
[GitHub](https://github.com/linda-mhmd/ai-solutions-wiki).

## Courses and learning paths

- **[The AI Film Crew Course](https://ai-solutions.wiki/course/).** How software
  and AI are built on AWS, told through one real system, the serverless AI video
  pipeline, and a garden metaphor. Nine modules, about 24 minutes, with video
  and full transcripts. The talk behind it is
  [From Raw Footage to Publish-Ready](/talks/aws-community-day-greece/).
- **[The Gardener Path](https://ai-solutions.wiki/learn/gardener/).** The cloud,
  serverless and orchestration explained the way you would grow a garden. Each
  lesson has a short video, a plain-words explanation with sources, and a quick
  check.
- **[The Garden Lens](https://ai-solutions.wiki/learn/garden-lens/).** The whole
  system as a garden, in short clips, each one a metaphor for one real idea.

## The method, written down

- **[From AI Idea to Working Prototype in 3 Workshops](https://ai-solutions.wiki/frameworks/three-workshop-method/).**
  The three-workshop format I run with clients, from AI curiosity to a
  validated, buildable prototype.
- **[The Use Case Scoring Framework](https://ai-solutions.wiki/frameworks/use-case-scoring/).**
  From 57 ideas to 3 prototypes: the scoring that decides what is worth
  building first.
- **[AI Systems Are Software Systems](https://ai-solutions.wiki/guides/ai-systems-are-software-systems/).**
  The argument the whole wiki is organised around.
- **[The Five Layers of AI Systems](https://ai-solutions.wiki/layers/).** The
  map I draw in almost every talk.

## Patterns from real builds

- **[Hybrid Cloud AI Video Pipeline](https://ai-solutions.wiki/solutions/media/hybrid-video-pipeline/).**
  A video pipeline that spans on-premises storage and AWS.
- **[Tiered Analysis Pattern](https://ai-solutions.wiki/patterns/tiered-analysis/).**
  Cheap analysis first, expensive analysis only for what passes the threshold.

Same technology, a different lens. These come from the things I do when I am
not building.

  [Juggling and Technology](https://ai-solutions.wiki/through/juggling-and-tech/),
  [Juggling and the Brain](https://ai-solutions.wiki/through/juggling-and-brain/)
  and
  [Juggling and Change](https://ai-solutions.wiki/through/juggling-and-change/).
- **[The Fashionista](https://ai-solutions.wiki/through/wardrobe/).** Version
  control and technical debt as seasonal collections and cluttered wardrobes.

## Why it is on a wiki

A talk reaches the people in the room. A wiki page reaches the person who
searches for the problem two years later. I wrote about the reasoning in
[Why I Built AI Solutions Wiki](https://www.lindamohamed.com/blog/free-open-source-ai-wiki).
