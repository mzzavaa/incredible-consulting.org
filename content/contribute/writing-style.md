**Purpose.** Keeps forty-odd artefacts sounding like one library. For contributors and for anyone maintaining a fork.

**Contains.**
- Voice rules: second person, present tense, concrete nouns, no superlatives
- Banned constructions with replacements, including em-dashes and marketing verbs
- Structure rules: purpose, contains, primary action, links, in that order
- Evidence rule: every claim either cites a source or names the engagement type it came from
- Terminology: use the glossary, do not invent parallel vocabulary
- Formatting: heading levels, table use, diagram conventions, and file naming
- Accessibility requirements for diagrams and downloads

**Primary CTA.** Read the banned-constructions list before your first draft.
