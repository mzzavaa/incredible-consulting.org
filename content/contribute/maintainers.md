**Purpose.** Puts names against the work. For contributors who need a decision, and for readers assessing whether the material has real authorship.

**Contains.**
- Maintainer table: person, area of the library, and contact handle
- Lead maintainer and the scope of decisions reserved to that role
- Reviewer pool with domains of expertise
- How to join: contribution track record, then invitation
- Time commitment expected of a maintainer, stated honestly
- Succession note: what happens to an area when a maintainer steps back

**Primary CTA.** Contact the maintainer for the area you want to work on.
**Links to.** /contribute/review-process/, /about/, https://incredible.community/.
