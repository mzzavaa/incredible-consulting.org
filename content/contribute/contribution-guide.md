**Purpose.** For first-time contributors who want to know the shape of the work before starting it. Reduces wasted effort on both sides.

**Contains.**
- Before you start: check open issues, claim the work, agree the scope
- Repository layout and where each artefact type lives
- Local setup: Hugo version, make targets, and preview instructions
- Acceptance criteria: field-tested, specific, and reusable outside its origin context
- What gets rejected: vendor promotion, untested theory, and rewritten marketing copy
- Licensing agreement for contributions, stated in two sentences
- Escalation path if a review stalls

**Primary CTA.** Claim an open issue before writing anything.
