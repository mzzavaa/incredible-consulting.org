**Purpose.** Makes the review predictable so contributors are not left guessing. Also documents how quality is defended without gatekeeping.

**Contains.**
- Review stages: triage, technical review, editorial review, publication
- Reviewer roles and how someone becomes a reviewer
- Criteria checklist applied at each stage
- Response time commitment and what to do when it is missed
- Disagreement handling: how a contested technical claim gets resolved
- Versioning and changelog entry on merge
- Post-publication corrections and how they are marked

**Primary CTA.** Expect a first response within ten working days, and chase if you do not get one.
