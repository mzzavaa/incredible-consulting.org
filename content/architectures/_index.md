**Purpose.** A reference set for architects who want a defensible starting point plus the reasoning behind it. Every entry states the problem, the pattern, and the conditions under which it is the wrong choice.

**Contains.**
- Grid rendered from `data/architectures.yaml` with problem statement, cloud coverage, and maturity
- Shared diagram conventions note: C4 levels, notation, and colour meaning
- "When not to use this" is a required section in every entry, surfaced on the card
- Downloadable source: diagram files plus infrastructure-as-code skeletons where they exist
- Vendor neutrality statement and how vendor-specific variants are labelled

**Primary CTA.** Take a diagram into your next architecture review.
