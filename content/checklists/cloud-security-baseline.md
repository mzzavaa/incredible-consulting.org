**Purpose.** A foundation-level gate for platform teams and a starting evidence set for auditors. It complements the landing zone architecture rather than repeating it.

**Contains.**
- Identity controls: workforce, workload, and privileged access
- Network and egress controls with a minimum acceptable configuration
- Logging, retention, and tamper resistance requirements
- Encryption and key management, including who can access keys
- Detection and response minimums for teams without a security operations centre
- Framework mapping columns for the most common audit regimes
- Deliberate exclusions: application security, covered elsewhere

**Primary CTA.** Score your estate and produce a gap list with owners.
