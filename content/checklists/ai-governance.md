**Purpose.** For teams who need to show a risk, legal, or works council function that an AI system was built responsibly. Written for practitioners, reviewed against European regulatory expectations.

**Contains.**
- Purpose and scope declaration: what the system is and is not allowed to do
- Data provenance and lawful basis section with evidence requirements
- Evaluation evidence: what was tested, against what, and by whom
- Human oversight design, including meaningful override rather than nominal override
- Transparency obligations toward affected people
- Logging and retention requirements for later investigation
- Caveat: this is a practitioner aid, not legal advice, with a pointer to when counsel is required

**Primary CTA.** Complete the evidence columns before the design review, not after.
