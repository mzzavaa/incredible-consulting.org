**Purpose.** Transparency page and, secondarily, the only place on this site with a commercial ask. It exists so readers can judge independence for themselves.

**Contains.**
- Current funding sources: self-funded hours, grants, and named sponsors
- Independence rules: sponsors see results at publication, never before, and never influence questions
- What sponsorship buys: acknowledgement and nothing else, stated plainly
- Grant interests: topics we would run with public or foundation funding
- Cost model showing roughly what a study costs to run
- Contact path for funders, universities, and public bodies

**Primary CTA.** Contact the programme if you want to fund independent work, not favourable work.
