**Purpose.** The archive. For analysts, journalists, students, and practitioners who need something citable rather than a vendor blog post.

**Contains.**
- Reverse chronological list with publication date, sample size, and headline finding
- Per report: summary, method note, limitations section, and download links
- Raw data availability status and the anonymisation approach used
- Correction log, because published corrections are part of the credibility
- Citation format block, ready to paste
- Reuse terms restated for the data specifically

**Primary CTA.** Download the data and check the finding yourself.
