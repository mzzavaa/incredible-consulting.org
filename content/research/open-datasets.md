**Purpose.** For researchers and students who want to reanalyse rather than re-quote. Publishing the data is the difference between research and marketing.

**Contains.**
- Dataset index with schema, row count, collection window, and licence
- Data dictionary per dataset, field by field
- Anonymisation and aggregation method, including suppressed fields and why
- Known limitations and sampling bias notes
- Example notebook showing how to reproduce one headline figure
- Request path for access to datasets that cannot be fully public

**Primary CTA.** Reproduce a published figure, then tell us if it does not hold.
