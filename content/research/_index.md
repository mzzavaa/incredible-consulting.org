**Purpose.** The research programme landing page. It exists to produce evidence the consulting practice and the wider community can both cite, with methodology transparent enough to be criticised.

**Contains.**
- Current and past reports rendered from `data/research.yaml`
- Methodology principles: sample disclosed, questions published, raw data released where consent allows
- Active studies with recruitment status and closing dates
- Citation block for each report, plus a DOI where one exists
- Funding disclosure line naming every sponsor and their influence (none on findings)
- Peer feedback channel for challenging a published result

**Primary CTA.** Read the latest report or join the next study cohort.
**Links to.** /research/reports/, /research/take-part/, /research/funding-and-sponsorship/.
