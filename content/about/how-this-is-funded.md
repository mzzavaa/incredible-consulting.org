**Purpose.** Full financial transparency, because independence claims are worthless without disclosure. For readers, funders, and public sector bodies assessing conflicts.

**Contains.**
- Funding mix: consulting revenue reinvested, research grants, and named sponsorships
- Hours accounting: roughly how much time goes into the library each year
- Firewall rules: sponsors and clients cannot commission, review, or block an artefact
- Vendor relationships disclosed, including community programme affiliations
- What would change if funding stopped, and what would not
- Annual transparency note with the current year figures

**Primary CTA.** Judge the independence yourself against the disclosed relationships.
