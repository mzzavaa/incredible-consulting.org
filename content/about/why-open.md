**Purpose.** The position piece. For sceptical readers wondering what a consultancy gains by publishing its own playbooks, and for other consultancies considering the same move.

**Contains.**
- The premise: the method is not the moat, the judgement in applying it is
- What being open changes: better-prepared clients, shorter discovery, fewer bad-fit engagements
- What it costs: real hours, public exposure to criticism, and the occasional copied deck
- Why criticism is the feature, an artefact nobody challenges is probably untested
- The line held: nothing published here is a teaser for a paid version
- An invitation to other consultancies to publish theirs

**Primary CTA.** If you run a consultancy, publish one playbook and see what happens.
