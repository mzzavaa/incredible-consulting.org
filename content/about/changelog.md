**Purpose.** Version discipline for a library that people fork and print. For anyone holding a downloaded copy who needs to know if it is stale.

**Contains.**
- Reverse chronological entries: date, artefact, version, and change summary
- Change classification: correction, clarification, addition, or breaking revision
- Breaking revision definition, the changes that invalidate a printed copy
- Contributor credit per entry
- Subscription options: RSS and a low-volume update list
- Deprecation notices for artefacts being retired, with successor links

**Primary CTA.** Subscribe to the feed so your forked copies do not drift.
