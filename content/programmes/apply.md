**Purpose.** A single, low-friction entry point. Long application forms select for organisations with grant writers, not for organisations with need.

**Contains.**
- Current window status: open or closed, with the next opening date
- Short form: organisation, the problem in three sentences, decision maker, and availability
- Scoring criteria shown before the form, not after
- What happens next, with dates for shortlist and decision
- Reapplication policy for unsuccessful applicants
- Alternative routes if you are not eligible, including the free library and the community domain

**Primary CTA.** Submit the short form during an open window.
**Links to.** /programmes/, /playbooks/, https://incredible.builders/.
