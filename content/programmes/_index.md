**Purpose.** The public-good delivery arm. Where the rest of the site gives away artefacts, this gives away time, with transparent limits so it stays sustainable.

**Contains.**
- Annual capacity stated as a number of days, and how much remains this year
- Three tracks: pro bono projects, non-profit and NGO support, public sector advisory
- Selection criteria published in advance, including what disqualifies an application
- What is provided and what is explicitly not: advice and design yes, ongoing operations no
- Past programme work with outcomes, named where the organisation consented
- Educator and student track pointing to the training and community domains
