**Purpose.** For organisations doing work worth doing that cannot fund consulting. It uses the standard three-workshop method rather than a diminished version.

**Contains.**
- What a pro bono engagement includes: the full three workshops and the decision memo
- Eligibility: mission, size, and inability to fund at commercial rates
- Commitments required from the recipient, including participant time and a decision maker
- What is not included: implementation, ongoing support, and tooling costs
- Publication expectation: an anonymised or named write-up feeds back into the library
- Selection timeline and how many applications are typically received

**Primary CTA.** Apply during an open window with a named decision maker.
