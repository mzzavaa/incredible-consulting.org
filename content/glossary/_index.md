**Purpose.** A shared vocabulary page. It exists because most workshop confusion is vocabulary confusion, and because vendors redefine terms constantly.

**Contains.**
- Alphabetical index with jump links and a search field
- Two definitions per term: one for a business audience, one for engineers
- "Often confused with" line on terms that are routinely swapped
- Vendor-name callout where a generic concept has been branded
- Source and date on definitions that track a moving standard
- Suggest-a-term path into the contribution process

**Primary CTA.** Send the relevant terms to workshop participants before session one.
