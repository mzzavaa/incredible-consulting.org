**Purpose.** For teams told to "do something with AI" and given no criteria. It replaces opinion-led selection with a scoring model that survives a steering committee.

**Contains.**
- Scope and non-goals: this assesses readiness, it does not build anything
- Interview guide with question sets for business owners, data owners, and platform engineers
- Use case register template with the eight fields that matter
- Scoring model: value, data availability, regulatory exposure, integration cost, reversibility
- Data readiness heatmap, a one-page artefact for the steering committee
- Common failure modes, including the "we already have the data" trap
- Worked example using an anonymised industrial client

**Primary CTA.** Download the register template and score three candidate use cases this week.
