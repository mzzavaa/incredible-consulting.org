**Purpose.** For programme leads who inherited a migration with a deadline and no sequencing logic. It is written for the planning phase, not the lift.

**Contains.**
- Portfolio discovery: how to inventory applications when documentation is missing
- Disposition model (retire, retain, rehost, replatform, refactor, repurchase) with decision criteria
- Wave sequencing by business risk, dependency depth, and team availability
- Cutover runbook template including rollback windows and comms
- Decommission ledger: the discipline that actually delivers the savings
- Programme anti-patterns: the pilot that never ends, the wave that carries the hardest app first

**Primary CTA.** Build the disposition table for your top twenty applications.
