**Purpose.** Removes the hesitation that stops people using open material. For consultancies, trainers, and internal teams who need certainty before putting our artefacts in front of a client.

**Contains.**
- Two-line summary: CC BY 4.0 for text and diagrams, MIT for code, attribution required
- What is explicitly allowed, including commercial use by competitors
- What is not covered: the brand name, the logo, and any client material
- Per-artefact licence table so nothing depends on assumption
- Third-party content notice for embedded icons, fonts, and diagrams
- Contact path for anything the licence does not cover

**Primary CTA.** Use it, credit it, and tell us where it went.
