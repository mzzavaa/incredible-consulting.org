**Purpose.** The detailed reference for anyone who has to clear reuse with a legal team. Written to be read once and then cited.

**Contains.**
- Licence per content type with a link to the canonical licence text
- Permitted uses spelled out: training delivery, client deliverables, internal standards, resale of services
- Derivative works: how to mark modifications so readers know what changed
- Data licence specifics, including redistribution of the raw datasets
- Warranty disclaimer and limitation, stated without legal theatre
- Version history of these terms, because licence changes are not retroactive

**Primary CTA.** Copy the attribution string and ship your derivative.
