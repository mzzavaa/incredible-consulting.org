**Purpose.** Two related questions answered in one place: how to credit correctly, and when using the name would imply an endorsement that does not exist.

**Contains.**
- Attribution strings for slides, documents, code headers, and web pages
- Minimum attribution: author, source URL, licence, and modification note
- Trademark position: the name and logo are not covered by the content licence
- Permitted references: "based on the Incredible three-workshop method" and similar factual statements
- Not permitted: implying partnership, certification, or endorsement
- Partner and certified-facilitator path for those who want a formal relationship

**Primary CTA.** Paste the correct attribution string into your deliverable.
